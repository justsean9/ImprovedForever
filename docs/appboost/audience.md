# Audience

Based on what the repo states (README.md, CHANGELOG.md, docs/feature-requests.md). Anything not stated is marked unknown.

## Who benefits
- WoW Forever players who play with a gamepad (Xbox, DualSense, Switch Pro), including on Mac (the dev environment is macOS; player platform split: unknown).
- Players who want quality-of-life: bag clean-up, item lookup, auction house, fishing, a cleaner HUD, all without a keyboard step.

## Pain points the addons address
- Native gamepad UI lacks item info, junk handling, a usable auction window, quick wheels, readable quest tracking.
- Keyboard-only steps in controller setups (the project rule: every UI fully usable with a pad).

## Where they hang out
- CurseForge (the addon's page and comments), Reddit (a player bug report from Reddit is recorded in docs/feature-requests.md; which subreddits: unknown), Facebook (social media images made for it, groups: unknown).
